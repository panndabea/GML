Motion Primitives

Three Languages of Motion

Each component captures a distinct physical gesture and maps it to collective meaning — turning a room full of devices into a single expressive instrument.

GROUNDSWELL

Sustained Forward Tilt

Lean your device forward like committing to a decision. Held conviction becomes collective momentum — a tide of shared intent visible across the room.

📐 15° threshold ⏱ 1.5 s hold 💡 Conviction
TREMOR

Rapid Lateral Oscillation

Micro-oscillations reveal tension without intention. When a room tremors together it signals what surveys cannot — the unfiltered emotional temperature of a crowd.

〰 3 – 7 Hz 📊 5° amplitude 💡 Tension
ORBIT

Slow Circular Rotation

Rotate your device like a conductor gathering threads. When the room orbits together individual synthesis becomes collective convergence — a phase lock of shared intent.

🔄 3 – 6 s/rev 📐 270° arc 💡 Alignment

About GML

Motion Beyond Voting

Most audience engagement tools ask people to click buttons. GML asks them to move — capturing continuous, analog, involuntary signals richer than any binary choice.

📐

Analog, Not Binary

Voting captures position. Groundswell captures conviction. A 30° lean held for 8 seconds means something different to a 16° lean for 2 seconds.

🫀

Involuntary Signals

Tremor captures affective state in the moment — the body's response to tension, not a post-hoc rating of how tense you felt.

🌀

Collective Intelligence

When 1 000 devices orbit in the same direction simultaneously, something real is happening. Phase lock is a signal no survey could capture.
