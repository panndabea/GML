Motion Primitives

Twelve Languages of Motion

Each component captures a distinct physical gesture and maps it to collective meaning — turning a room full of devices into a single expressive instrument.

GROUNDSWELL

Sustained Forward Tilt

Lean your device forward like committing to a decision. Held conviction becomes collective momentum — a tide of shared intent visible across the room.

📐 15° threshold ⏱ 1.5 s hold 💡 Conviction
TREMOR

Rapid Lateral Oscillation

Micro-oscillations reveal tension without intention. When a room tremors together it signals what surveys cannot — the unfiltered emotional temperature of a crowd.

〰 3 – 7 Hz 📊 5° amplitude 💡 Tension
ORBIT

Slow Circular Rotation

Rotate your device like a conductor gathering threads. When the room orbits together individual synthesis becomes collective convergence — a phase lock of shared intent.

🔄 3 – 6 s/rev 📐 270° arc 💡 Alignment
ANCHOR

Complete Sustained Stillness

Hold your device perfectly motionless. When a room goes still together it signals what debate cannot — collective resolution and the quiet certainty of a made-up mind.

⬛ < 1° deviation ⏱ 3 s hold 💡 Resolve
SURGE

Sharp Upward Snap

Snap your device upward in a burst of energy. Collective surges timestamp excitement to the exact content moment that triggered it — precision no applause meter can match.

⚡ ≥ 120°/s ⏱ < 0.5 s snap 💡 Excitement
PULSE

Rhythmic Forward-Back Nod

Rock your device slowly forward and back like nodding. Sustained collective pulse creates a shared waveform — agreement made visible as a living wave across the room.

〰 0.5 – 1.5 Hz 📐 8 – 20° arc 💡 Agreement
PIVOT

Sharp 90° Yaw Rotation

Snap your device a quarter-turn and hold. Collective pivots mark the precise moment a room changes its mind — the first tool that timestamps opinion change rather than just final position.

🔄 80 – 100° snap ⏱ 1.5 s hold 💡 Reframing
CASCADE

Progressively Deepening Tilt

Let your device tilt ever further forward, slow and unstoppable. The escalation index captures not just how the crowd feels but how fast that feeling is accelerating.

📈 ≥ 25° deepening ⏱ 3 – 10 s ramp 💡 Escalation
LEAN

Sustained Left Tilt

Tilt your device to the left and hold. A collective lean signals measured scepticism or the weight of an alternative option pulling the room sideways — not a rejection, but a pause.

📐 15° threshold ⏱ 1.5 s hold 💡 Scepticism
BANK

Sustained Right Tilt

Tilt your device to the right and hold. A collective bank signals confident commitment — the room tilting decisively toward an idea, like an aircraft banking into a turn it intends to complete.

📐 15° threshold ⏱ 1.5 s hold 💡 Commitment
VEER

Sustained Left Twist

Rotate your device counterclockwise and hold. A collective veer marks deliberate redirection — the room signalling dissent or demanding a course change before momentum carries things too far.

🔄 40 – 60° CCW ⏱ 1.5 s hold 💡 Redirection
SWEEP

Sustained Right Twist

Rotate your device clockwise and hold. A collective sweep signals decisive clearance — the room turning right together as a unified act of moving forward, brushing aside doubt or hesitation.

🔄 40 – 60° CW ⏱ 1.5 s hold 💡 Clearance

About GML

Motion Beyond Voting

Most audience engagement tools ask people to click buttons. GML asks them to move — capturing continuous, analog, involuntary signals richer than any binary choice.

📐

Analog, Not Binary

Voting captures position. Groundswell captures conviction. A 30° lean held for 8 seconds means something different to a 16° lean for 2 seconds.

🫀

Involuntary Signals

Tremor captures affective state in the moment — the body's response to tension, not a post-hoc rating of how tense you felt.

🌀

Collective Intelligence

When 1 000 devices orbit in the same direction simultaneously, something real is happening. Phase lock is a signal no survey could capture.
